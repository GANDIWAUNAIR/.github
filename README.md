## 📌 About Gandiwa UNAIR

Gandiwa UNAIR is the robotics team from Universitas Airlangga dedicated to research, design, and innovation in unmanned vehicles. The team develops autonomous fixed-wing aircraft and remote-controlled surface vessels to represent Universitas Airlangga in national robotics competitions across Indonesia.

<br>

## 🏆 Competition Divisions

### ✈️ KRTI — Racing Plane Division (Akasa Gandiwa)
The **Kontes Robot Terbang Indonesia (KRTI)** Racing Plane team focuses on high-speed autonomous fixed-wing aircraft.
* **Focus:** Dynamic aerodynamic airframe design, rapid course navigation, high-speed stability, and real-time flight telemetry.
* **Objective:** Achieve maximum airspeed while navigating precision racing circuits and pylon turns.

### 🛥️ KKI — Electronic Remote Control Division (Aruna Gandiwa)
The **Kontes Kapal Indonesia (KKI)** Electronic Remote Control team engineers high-performance radio-controlled marine vessels.
* **Focus:** Hull hydrodynamics, high-efficiency propulsion mixing, responsive signal handling, and maneuverability.
* **Objective:** Execute fast obstacle navigation and sprint laps across water tracks.

<br>

## 🛠️ Core Focus Areas

* **Aerodynamics & Hull Design:** Custom 3D modeling, lightweight composite materials, and CFD simulation.
* **Avionics & Embedded Control:** Flight controller calibration, signal decoding, and safety fail-safes.
* **Telemetry & Field Operations:** Real-time metrics monitoring, ground control station management, and mission execution.
